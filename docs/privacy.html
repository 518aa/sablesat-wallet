Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日 · 适用于 SableSat iOS / Android 应用及官方网站

Effective: October 27, 2026 · Applies to the SableSat iOS / Android app and its website

一句话总结：SableSat 是非托管钱包。你的助记词、私钥和联系人只保存在本机；开发者不设账户系统、不收集身份信息、不投放广告、不接入分析或追踪 SDK，也无法动用或查看你的资产。

1. 我们是谁

本政策中的“我们”指 SableSat 应用的开发者。SableSat 是一款开源的比特币自托管钱包，同时支持多签金库、离线签名和部分 EVM 链资产。使用本应用无需注册账号。

2. 我们不收集的信息

开发者不运营用于存储用户数据的后端服务器，也不收集以下信息：

  • 姓名、邮箱、手机号、身份证件或任何 KYC 资料；
  • 账户、密码或登录凭证（应用内没有账号体系）；
  • 设备标识符（IDFA / 广告 ID）用于跨应用追踪；
  • 使用行为、页面浏览、崩溃日志或诊断数据；
  • 通讯录、照片、精确位置或麦克风数据。

应用未集成任何第三方分析、广告、归因或崩溃上报 SDK。

3. 仅保存在你设备上的数据

以下数据由应用在本地生成并保存，不会上传至开发者或任何我们控制的服务器：

  • 助记词、私钥、扩展公钥与多签描述符：加密后存储于系统安全存储（iOS Keychain / Secure Enclave 门禁，Android Keystore）。加密算法为 AES-256-GCM；密钥材料受 PIN 与生物识别保护。
  • 联系人：你添加的收款联系人姓名与地址，加密后保存在应用私有存储中。
  • 设置与缓存：网络模式、主题、交易历史缓存、汇率缓存等，保存在应用私有存储中。

卸载应用或在应用内重置钱包会删除上述本地数据。请注意：删除后无法恢复，助记词与多签描述符的备份由你自行负责。应用不会把你的备份上传到云端；如你使用 iCloud / Google 备份，该行为由操作系统与你的账户设置决定，与开发者无关。

4. 联网时会发生什么

为了查询余额、获取手续费、广播交易和显示价格，应用会直接从你的设备向第三方公共服务发起 HTTPS 请求。这些请求不经过开发者服务器，但第三方服务商必然会接收到你的 IP 地址、请求时间，以及所查询的公开链上数据（如地址、交易 ID、广播的原始交易、代币标识）。

5. 设备权限

  • 相机：仅用于扫描二维码（收款地址、PSBT、协同方公钥）。图像在本机实时解析，不保存、不上传。
  • 生物识别：通过 iOS Face ID / Touch ID 或 Android 生物识别 API 解锁钱包与授权签名。应用仅收到“验证成功/失败”的结果，无法访问你的指纹或面部数据。
  • 剪贴板：仅在你主动点击“复制”时写入地址或交易信息；应用不会在后台读取剪贴板。
  • 网络：用于上述区块链与价格查询。

6. 数据共享与出售

我们不出售、不出租、不交换你的个人信息，也不会为营销目的共享任何数据。除第 4 节所述的必要链上查询外，应用不会向第三方传输数据。

7. 安全措施

应用采用本地加密、系统安全存储、PIN 指数退避、防截屏、越狱 / Root 检测与交易防篡改校验等措施。但请理解：自托管意味着资产安全最终取决于你如何保管助记词、PIN 与备份。任何安全措施都无法消除全部风险。

8. 儿童

SableSat 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。由于应用不收集任何身份信息，如你认为相关数据被误处理，请通过下方方式联系我们。

9. 你的权利

由于开发者不持有你的个人数据，通常不存在可供查询、更正或删除的服务器端记录。你可以随时：

  • 在应用内删除联系人、重置钱包；
  • 卸载应用以删除全部本地数据；
  • 通过下方联系方式询问本政策或数据处理相关问题。

如你所在司法辖区（如欧盟 GDPR、美国州隐私法）赋予你额外权利，我们会在适用范围内予以尊重；但因我们不保存个人数据，多数请求在技术上无对应数据可操作。

10. 国际传输

开发者不传输你的数据。你直接访问的第三方区块链与价格服务可能位于你所在国家/地区之外，相关请求受这些服务商的隐私政策约束。

11. 政策变更

我们可能因功能或法规变化更新本政策。更新后的版本会发布在本页面并修改生效日期；涉及重大变化时，我们会在应用或 GitHub 仓库中提示。继续使用应用即表示你接受更新后的政策。

12. 联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，或希望行使上述权利，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们：github.com/518aa/sablesat-wallet/issues。我们通常会在 7 个工作日内回复。

In one sentence: SableSat is a non-custodial wallet. Your seed phrase, private keys and contacts stay on your device. The developer has no account system, collects no identity data, serves no ads, and integrates no analytics or tracking SDKs — and cannot access or move your funds.

1. Who we are

"We" in this policy means the developer of SableSat. SableSat is an open-source Bitcoin self-custody wallet that also supports multisig vaults, air-gapped signing and select EVM-chain assets. No account is required to use the app.

2. What we do not collect

The developer does not operate a backend that stores user data and does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, government IDs or any KYC information;
  • accounts, passwords or login credentials (there is no account system);
  • device identifiers (IDFA / advertising ID) for cross-app tracking;
  • usage behavior, page views, crash logs or diagnostics;
  • contacts, photos, precise location or microphone data.

The app includes no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting SDK.

3. Data stored only on your device

The following is generated and kept locally and is never uploaded to the developer or any server we control:

  • Seed phrase, private keys, extended public keys and multisig descriptors: encrypted and stored in the platform secure store (iOS Keychain / Secure Enclave gating, Android Keystore) using AES-256-GCM, protected by your PIN and biometrics.
  • Contacts: names and addresses you add, encrypted in the app's private storage.
  • Settings and caches: network mode, theme, transaction-history and price caches in the app's private storage.

Uninstalling the app or resetting the wallet in-app deletes this local data. Deletion is irreversible; backing up your seed phrase and multisig descriptor is your responsibility. The app does not upload backups to the cloud. If you use iCloud or Google device backups, that is governed by your OS and account settings, not by the developer.

4. What happens when the app goes online

To fetch balances, fees and prices and to broadcast transactions, the app sends HTTPS requests directly from your device to third-party public services. These requests do not pass through a developer server, but those providers necessarily receive your IP address, request time and the public on-chain data being queried (such as addresses, transaction IDs, raw transactions being broadcast and token identifiers).

5. Device permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes (payment addresses, PSBTs, cosigner public keys). Images are parsed on-device in real time; they are not stored or uploaded.
  • Biometrics: unlock and signature approval via iOS Face ID / Touch ID or Android Biometric API. The app only receives a success/failure result and cannot access your fingerprints or facial data.
  • Clipboard: written only when you tap "Copy" to copy an address or transaction detail. The app does not read the clipboard in the background.
  • Network: for the blockchain and price queries described above.

6. Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and do not share data for marketing. Apart from the necessary on-chain queries described in Section 4, the app transmits no data to third parties.

7. Security

The app uses local encryption, platform secure storage, PIN rate-limiting, screenshot protection, jailbreak/root detection and transaction tamper checks. Please understand that self-custody means the security of your assets ultimately depends on how you protect your seed phrase, PIN and backups. No safeguard eliminates all risk.

8. Children

SableSat is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Because the app collects no identity data, if you believe any data has been mishandled, please contact us below.

9. Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data, there are generally no server-side records to access, correct or delete. You may at any time:

  • delete contacts or reset the wallet in the app;
  • uninstall the app to remove all local data;
  • contact us below with questions about this policy or our data practices.

If your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in the EU or U.S. state privacy laws) grants you additional rights, we respect them to the extent applicable; however, since we store no personal data, most requests will have no corresponding data to act on.

10. International transfers

The developer transfers no data. The third-party blockchain and price services you contact directly may be located outside your country; those requests are governed by their privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features or regulations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date; for material changes we will note them in the app or the GitHub repository. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy or to exercise your rights, contact us via GitHub Issues: github.com/518aa/sablesat-wallet/issues. We usually respond within 7 business days.
